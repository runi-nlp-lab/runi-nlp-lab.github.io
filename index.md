<h2 class="runi-section-title">Our Research</h2>

<div class="research-grid">

  <div class="research-card">
    <h3 class="research-card-title">Foundational NLP</h3>
    <p class="research-card-desc">We investigate core challenges in NLP, including LLM analysis, text representation, temporal reasoning, and efficient text generation. Our work explores how meaning and reasoning emerge within state-of-the-art neural architectures.</p>
    <p class="research-card-example"><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.117/">Large Temporal Models: Unlocking Temporal Understanding in LLMs for Temporal Relation Classification</a> <span class="research-card-cite">(Homburger and Bar, 2025)</span></p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-card">
    <h3 class="research-card-title">Healthcare</h3>
    <p class="research-card-desc">We develop NLP systems with real-world healthcare applications, from pipelines that extract structured patient journeys from clinical records to language models for automated mental health therapy.</p>
    <p class="research-card-example"><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.103/">Decode Like a Clinician: Enhancing LLM Fine-Tuning with Temporal Structured Data Representation</a> <span class="research-card-cite">(Fadlon et al., 2025)</span></p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-card">
    <h3 class="research-card-title">Digital Humanities</h3>
    <p class="research-card-desc">We develop language technologies for the analysis of diverse historical corpora, enabling large-scale computational access to texts in under-resourced languages like Sanskrit and Tibetan.</p>
    <p class="research-card-example"><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.114/">DharmaBench: Evaluating Language Models on Buddhist Texts in Sanskrit and Tibetan</a> <span class="research-card-cite">(Hashiloni et al., 2025)</span></p>
  </div>
